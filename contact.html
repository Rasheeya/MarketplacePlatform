Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate answers to common questions about orders, escrow safety, returns, and seller operations.

How does the Stackly Escrow Protection work for buyers?
When you complete an order, your payment is securely deposited into our audited escrow vault. The vendor receives a fulfillment notice to pack and dispatch your order. The funds are only released to the seller after the courier confirms delivery and your 14-day inspection period elapses without dispute.
What are the requirements to become a verified seller?
Sellers must provide registered business licenses, tax identification numbers, verified identity credentials (KYC), and undergo a quality sample check. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy against counterfeit or replica items.
How does product comparison work on the website?
Simply click the exchange/compare icon on any product card across the catalog. You can add up to 3 items at a time. The bottom floating comparison bar will display an interactive modal comparing technical specs, warranty terms, and seller ratings side-by-side.
What is the return and refund policy?
We provide an unconditional 14-day return window on eligible items. If an item arrives damaged, defective, or misdescribed, you can initiate a return directly from your User Dashboard with a pre-paid courier label. Instant refunds are processed upon item scan.
Can corporate clients make bulk purchases with tax invoices?
Yes! Corporate buyers can register for a Client Account. The Client Dashboard provides access to volume discount tiers, automated tax invoicing, multi-user procurement approval flows, and customized escrow wire transfers.
